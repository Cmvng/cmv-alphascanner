Victor Damilola Bukola-Ojumu

Artist · Trader · Product Builder · Operations & Sales

Multidisciplinary operator, artist and product builder based in Lagos, Nigeria. Managed inventory, sales records and an eight-person team at a car dealership; founded and ran a 10-pond commercial catfish farm; ran outdoor charcoal barbecue cooking for two years; and has traded forex and crypto markets since 2019. A self-taught graphite and charcoal artist with two collections on OpenSea, and the builder of live web products, including an AI football-prediction platform and an automated forex and crypto signal bot. Civil Engineering graduate of the Federal University Oye-Ekiti.
