About

Pencil, charcoal, and the questions that won't let go.

CMVNG makes detailed drawings and digital works. The same questions keep coming back: power and who holds it, war and what it leaves behind, the daily struggle for meaning and purpose, and love as the thing worth reaching for.

Many of the pieces here are also collectible on OpenSea. New works and works in progress are added straight from the studio.
