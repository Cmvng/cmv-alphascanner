# Research 1: motivational speech edits, the craft (22 Oct)

Bottom line: the evidence backs restraint. Use the owner's own footage, clean sync, and no stacked effects or SFX.

## Building the edit
- Lay the speech on the timeline first, then cut the picture to it.
  - Cut in the breath pauses, never mid-word. [CineD 2017]
  - Cut on the music's hits, every 2 beats (or every 4 for a cinematic feel).
- Choose each shot for the feeling of the line, not its literal words. Walter Murch's Rule of Six weights emotion 51%, story 23%, rhythm 10%.
  - For example, "burning" gets a close-up of the hand pressing charcoal, not a flame overlay.
- Don't fill every pause with text: silence is part of the delivery.

## Captions
- **Font:** serif suits the genre (Garamond, Bodoni, Times; TikTok's own serif in "book quote" edits). Keep serif lines on black or on still frames; they are hard to read over busy motion.
- **Script accent:** at most one word per line, the word that changes the meaning, shown as it is spoken.
- **Size** at 1080 × 1920:
  - bold motivational text runs 75–95 px (vendor source);
  - our template 4 measured smaller, about 40–46 px serif on black: an understated, premium look.
- **Timing:** one line (two at most), on screen from at least 5/6 s up to 7 s. It appears on the first spoken word and leaves before the next beat.
- **Safe zones:**
  - TikTok: 108 px clear at the top, 320 px at the bottom, 60 px on the left and 120 px on the right.
  - Reels: 320 px clear at the bottom and 84 px on the right.
  - Lines centred in the frame stay clear of all of these.

## Hook and ending
- Instagram's Skip Rate metric (Aug 2025) counts views that leave within 3 s. Open on the strongest frame or line, with no intro.
- Progress videos need a clear start and an obvious finished state (template 2: hook text → hard cuts between stages → reveal).
- End on the reveal or the last line, then cut to black.

## Quality vs "slop"
- No stacked effects or heavy filters; at most 2–3 transition types. Too many sound effects make an edit feel busy.
- Mosseri (Jan 2026): "Flattering imagery is cheap to produce and boring to consume… imperfection becomes a signal."
- Instagram counts text overlays, narration and a storyline as original. Borders, watermarks and speed changes alone are not.
- Don't upscale (it gives an over-sharpened look). Put all text in before export (text added inside Instagram forces a re-encode). Export at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, and upload at highest quality.

## Audio rights
- Meta matches Reel audio against rights holders' files: the Reel can be muted, removed, shown less or blocked. A credit is not a licence.
- Instagram library music is for personal, non-commercial use, and posts promoting Gamdom are commercial. The music bed under a speech is the likelier trigger.
- Practical route:
  - export without sound, then add the template's sound in the app with "Use audio", which credits the source;
  - if the Reel is muted anyway, "Replace audio" swaps in a library track.
- TikTok scans audio on upload; business accounts get only the Commercial Music Library.
