## The story behind the tagline
Gamdom was hacked and offline from 13 to 16 Oct; "BACK Online" was posted on 16 Oct. The challenge and "Must fight back when kicked down" went up hours later. A castle (Gamdom's logo is a castle tower) rising from the ashes = Gamdom coming back after being knocked down.
