# The Living Drawing (9 Nov 2026): post kit

**Concept:** your Gamdom charcoal drawing wakes up. The real footage first (the tools, the hand, the hours, you facing
the eye), "It started as dust.", one beat of dark, then on the drop the camera goes inside the drawing: light moves
over the cracked letters, the eye catches it, the tear falls into the ruins, the castle's flags move, and it pulls
back to the whole sheet.

**The signature moment:** "the eye". The light comes up across the word and the eye is suddenly looking back.

## Be straight about how it was made
- The drawing is yours, by hand, and every tone on screen is a photo of it. Your footage is not regraded.
- The moving light, the depth and the camera are computer-made from those photos, and two shots (the letters and the
  castle) use software depth estimation. If someone asks "is this AI?", the honest answer is: the drawing is not,
  the 3D lighting is software. Don't caption it "no AI".
