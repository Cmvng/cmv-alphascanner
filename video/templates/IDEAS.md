# Idea bank: so no two videos look the same

The owner (5 Oct 2026): "It's very important you make sure the videos are different and not generic. The social media
algo doesn't like it." The owner keeps sending references (`talking-head/README.md`, "Inspiration") and expects ideas of
our own too. Pick from here, build what's missing, and add new ideas as they come. Mark an idea with the date once used.

The renderer stops a new video whose run of screens is within two changes of one of the last three of its kind in the
same look (`video/.cache/variety/history.json`). That is the floor, not the goal. The 1–5 Oct analysis videos all ran
cold open → hook → stakes → players → tactics → model → verdict → round-up, one screen added each day: that is what the
owner means by generic.

## Every video changes all five
1. **Format:** the shape of the story (below), not just the order of screens.
2. **Hook:** a question, a number, a myth, a countdown, a quote, a "what if". Never the same kind twice running.
3. **The main visual device:** one thing the video is remembered for (a pitch map, a race, a receipt, a front page).
4. **Look:** style (stadium night, broadcast graphics, newspaper, chalkboard, retro TV, clean white UI), always blue.
5. **Sound and pace:** a new track, a different cut rhythm, voice or no voice, one voice or two.

## Pre-match analysis: formats
- **One player's night:** the whole match through one player's story (e.g. Olise carrying France without Mbappé).
- **The duel:** two players or two teams, numbers racing side by side in bars, round by round.
- **Three questions:** the video asks three questions the match will answer and ends on a cliffhanger; the post-match
  video answers them (a series viewers come back for).
- **Myth v numbers:** "Everyone says X. The numbers say Y." Three myths, each busted or confirmed.
- **The map:** a top-down pitch where arrows and heat zones show where each side attacks and where it's weak.
- **What's on the line, live:** the group table animates for each result (win, draw, loss) so the stakes are seen.
- **Countdown to kick-off:** five facts, 5 → 1, the last one the twist.
- **The rivalry strip:** a scrolling timeline of the past meetings, landing on tonight.
- **The newspaper:** tomorrow's two possible front pages (one per winner); the analysis decides which is likelier.
- **Two voices:** two presenters disagree, each with their numbers; the model settles it.

## Post-match: formats
- **Our call v what happened:** a scoreboard of each call we made, ticked or crossed, with the honest why.
- **The turning point:** one moment, frozen and explained in our own redrawn graphics.
- **Minute by minute:** a timeline strip that fills in as the goals and cards land.
- **What we got wrong:** an honesty format when the read failed; it builds trust.
- **Ratings wall:** the players as cards, the best and worst flipped over last.

## The owner's own recordings (crypto, Polymarket)
- The two creators' look (4 Oct): the owner full frame, screens floating in as tilted cards, numbers popping out,
  white list cards, full-screen walkthroughs with the owner in a corner.
- The TikTok ideas (5 Oct): kinetic words on white or black for the key beats ("CANCEL", "$14"), our own redrawn UI
  that builds (a combo slip leg by leg, a button tapped), handwritten notes and arrows, numbered tiles, a cliffhanger end.
- **The receipt:** the combo or ticket prints out like a receipt, line by line, the payout at the bottom.
- **Split decision:** two choices on screen, the owner picks; the pause before the pick is the suspense.
- **The live chart:** the price line draws itself while the owner talks, the levels they name snapping on.
- **Before / after:** the same screen yesterday and today, wiped across.
