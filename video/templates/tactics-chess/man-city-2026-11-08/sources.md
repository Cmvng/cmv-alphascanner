# Nottingham Forest 3-1 Man City and three defeats in a row: sources (checked 8 Nov 2026)

Every fact in the script and on screen, with where it comes from. Source keys are listed at the bottom.

## Facts on screen and in the script

**The run: 7 wins, then 3 defeats (Aston Villa 2-1 City, City 0-1 Brighton, Forest 3-1 City)** [FPL][SKY-V][SKY-B][SKY-R]
- City's goals: 19-7 in the seven wins, 2-6 in the three defeats. The Brighton score is from the same FPL fixture data.
- **"No top-flight team in 122 years"** [SKY-R]: "the first top-flight team in 122 years to win their first seven and lose the next three".

**The match (7 Nov 2026, City Ground)** [SKY-S][SKY-T][ESPN-G]
- **Shape:** Forest 3-4-2-1, City 4-2-3-1. Formations are from the ESPN team sheet; the line-ups and shirt numbers are from Sky.
- **Ball and passes:** possession 78% City v 22% Forest; passes 753 v 206 [SKY-S].
- **Running:** Forest ran "six or seven kilometres more" (Glasner) [ESPN-M]; Sky puts it at about 6 km [SKY-R].
- **Interceptions:** Forest 11, City 1 [SKY-S].

**Goal 1, Jair Cunha, 6'** [SKY-R][PA][NBC][ESPN-G]
- City were building from the back. Cunha (Forest's right centre-back) "tracked" Gvardiol and took the ball off him "high up the pitch".
- He carried it into the box past Guéhi and finished low.

**Goal 2, Liam Delap, 18', assist Schlager** [PA][SKY-R][LS][EK][ESPN-NS][NBC]
- A loose ball: Ndiaye and Anderson "left the ball to each other". Schlager beat Anderson to it and played Delap through.
- Delap ran from inside his own half, between Dias and Guéhi.

**Rayan Cherki, 52'** [PA][ESPN-G][ESPN-NS][LS]
- Doku crossed from the left, the cross was deflected, and Cherki scored with a half-volley from the edge of the area.
- Who played the ball to Doku is unconfirmed, so the board starts at Doku.

**Goal 3, Delap, 73', assist Gibbs-White** [FF][SKY-R][PA][EK][ESPN-G]
- City "lost the duel at the edge of the box" (Maresca).
- Delap released Gibbs-White in Forest's own half, and the two ran the length of the pitch. Gibbs-White passed left, and Delap scored off the far post.
- Who lost the duel is not named in any source; the board shows a City midfielder.

**Maresca: "We lost all of them"** (the duels and second balls) [GOAL], 7 Nov 2026.

**The pattern** [PL][Sky stats pages][FPL]
- City conceded inside the first 11 minutes in all three defeats [PL].
- Across the three games: shots 48 v 26, xG 4.96 v 2.67, goals 2 v 6 [SKY-VS][SKY-BS][SKY-S]. Sky does not name its xG provider.

**Haaland and Semenyo** [FPL][ESPN-H]
- Haaland has 9 Premier League goals. He scored in every game from GW2 to GW8 and in neither GW9 nor GW10.
- Semenyo has a knee injury with an unknown return date (FPL news, 17 Oct). He played in all seven wins and none of the three defeats.

**"Losing the transitions"** is our read. It is backed by Maresca calling the second and third goals "copy and paste" transitions [FF], and by Sky: "the blueprint for stopping them is now becoming clear" [SKY-R].
