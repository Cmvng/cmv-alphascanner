# Pose prompts for a talking character (17 Nov 2026)

The owner makes the character images themselves (their words, 17 Nov: "Send me the prompt of the different poses for
the characters.... So I create them"). We animate them in our own page: a pose per line, the mouth switching between
closed and open on every word. A girl by default, because Lara voices her (2 Oct: no male presenter voice).

## How the images must come
- **The same character every time:** make the base image first, then attach it to every pose prompt as the reference.
- **Full body, head to shoes,** with a little space above the head and below the feet, at the same size and position
  in every image, so a pose change doesn't make her jump.
- **Plain pure white background,** no shadow, no text, no props unless the pose has one. We cut it out.
- **Face towards the viewer,** so the mouth is visible. The screens will be on the right, so she stands slightly
  turned to the right.
- **Portrait 2:3, high resolution** (1024×1536 or more).
- **Two versions of every pose:** mouth closed, and mouth open (the edit prompt at the end).

## 1. The base character
A 2D cartoon illustration of a friendly young woman presenter, full body from head to shoes, standing and facing the
viewer, turned slightly towards the right side of the picture. Soft painted digital illustration with clean outlines and
gentle shading, like a modern explainer-video character. She wears a royal-blue hoodie, light beige cargo trousers and
white sneakers. Warm, smart, approachable face, mouth closed in a small smile. Plain pure white background, no shadow,
no text, no props. Portrait 2:3, high resolution, character centred, a little space above her head and below her feet.

## 2. Every pose: start with this line, then add the pose
Use the attached image as the exact same character: same face, hair, skin, clothes, colours, art style, size and
position. Full body, head to shoes, feet at the same height as in the reference. Plain pure white background, no
shadow, no text. Mouth closed. Pose:

| # | Pose (paste after the line above) | For lines like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | standing relaxed, arms down by her sides, small smile | normal talking |
| 2 | thinking: one hand on her chin, eyes looking up, curious face | a question ("What does a 50% price mean?") |
| 3 | explaining: one hand open, palm up at chest height, friendly face | explaining a point |
| 4 | pointing with one arm straight out to the right side of the picture, looking the same way | "look at this" (the screen beside her) |
| 5 | one index finger raised up, eyebrows up, "I've got it" face | the key point |
| 6 | holding up two fingers, counting | "two things" |
| 7 | shrugging, both palms up, eyebrows raised | "it depends" |
| 8 | surprised: both hands on her cheeks, eyes wide | a shocking number |
| 9 | arms crossed, confident smile | the verdict |
| 10 | thumbs up, big smile | "that's how it works" |
| 11 | holding a magnifying glass up to one eye, looking right | checking a number or a fact |
| 12 | holding a smartphone in both hands, looking at it, smiling | "open the app" |
| 13 | one hand raised, palm out like "stop", serious face | a risk or a warning |
| 14 | waving hello with one hand, big smile | the opening and the follow line |
| 15 | hands in her hoodie pocket, relaxed, looking right | while a screen is showing |

## 3. The open-mouth version of each pose
Edit the attached image: keep everything exactly the same (pose, face, clothes, size, position, background). Only open
her mouth naturally, as if she is in the middle of saying a word.

(Optional, for smoother talking: one more with the mouth slightly open, and one with a wide open "O" mouth.)

## Notes
- For a project's video, change the hoodie colour to the project's colour (e.g. orange for Primus) in the base prompt.
- To change her look, describe it in the base prompt (skin, hair, glasses).
- ChatGPT's image tool and Google Gemini both keep a character well when the base image is attached.
