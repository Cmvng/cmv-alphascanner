## How a combo works on Bagel (the how-to)
- Every pick has to win. Bagel's FAQ (Trading & Settlement, edited 5 Nov 2026, bagelwin.notion.site Help Center) says: "Final payout = max payout ×
  the product of each leg's result". A loss counts 0, so one loss pays $0.
- A combo is two or more outcomes: docs.polymarket.com/trading/combos/overview.md, read 7 Nov 2026.
- Download: "Bagel - Predict on easy mode" (GM AI Singapore). On iOS, apps.apple.com/ng/app/bagel-predict-on-easy-mode/id6760811152
  (v1.6.0, 6 Nov 2026). On Android, play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=win.bagel (updated 3 Nov 2026).
- The labels on screen ("Add Funds", "Yes", "Cost", "Max payout", "3 Pick Combo 5.37x") come from the owner's own screenshot. "Build
  a Combo" and "Combo 1 pick" are from the owner's 29 Oct screens.
- Who can use it: 18+ (Terms §1.1). Blocked countries are listed in Terms §1.2 (bagel.win/terms-of-service, last updated 25 May 2026,
  re-checked 7 Nov). Nigeria is not on the list.
- Fees: 1% on market orders and 0.5% on limit orders (Terms §3.2). It is not confirmed which one a combo order pays.
