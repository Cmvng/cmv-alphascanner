# Sources (Bagel, 3 Nov 2026)

- **Fees: "Market orders are charged a 1% fee. Limit orders are charged a 0.5% fee."**
  - Bagel's Terms of Service §3.2, Last Updated 25 May 2026: https://bagel.win/terms-of-service
  - The same Terms say the fee is "automatically deducted at the time of execution". So $9 at 45¢ with a 1% market
    order is about 19.8 shares. That's why the $14 and $4 are "about", and the example is marked "my example".
- **What Bagel is:** a mobile app on Polymarket's markets, listed as a verified builder at https://builders.polymarket.com
  (read 29 Oct 2026).
  - iOS: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bagel-predict-on-easy-mode/id6760811152
  - Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=win.bagel
- **Logo, icon and app image:** from bagel.win (`/static/svgs/logo-horizontal.svg`, `/favicon.svg`,
  `/static/images/home-logo-v1.png`).
- **Steps 1–4:** the owner's own pictures (`pics/`).
- **The example's maths:**
  - $9 ÷ 45¢ = 20 shares;
  - 20 × 70¢ = $14;
  - 20 × 20¢ = $4;
  - at full time, 20 × $1 = $20, or $0.
