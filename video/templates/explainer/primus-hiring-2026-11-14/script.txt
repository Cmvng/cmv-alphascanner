Let me explain how Primus Labs tech works, with one practical use case.

You apply for a remote job. The team only needs a few answers. Do you have this background? Do you have this credential?

But they ask for your full C V, your certificates, even your I D. Now they have your phone number, your address, and everything that was never part of the question.

With Primus, you open the real website yourself, and Z K T L S turns that logged-in session into a proof of one fact. The attestor watches the encrypted session, never your login.

The hiring app gets one line: this credential, true. Not your whole file.

The official docs even list this under hiring.

One limit though. The proof shows what the website showed at that time, not that the account is uniquely you. And it's a use case for apps to build on, not a job board you can apply on today.

That's all for now.
