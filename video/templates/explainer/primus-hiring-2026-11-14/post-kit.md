whole file: your CV, your certificates and your ID fly into the team's folder, and out spill your phone number, your

page with "identity, academic background, skills" ringed, and the Developer Hub.

## X (your own post, as you wrote it)
let me explain a practical usecase of how the @primus_labs tech works in real time.....

you apply for a remote job, and the team only needs a few answers....

do you have this background
do you have this credential

but what they ask for is your full cv, your certificates, sometimes your id, and a login to your profile.... now they have your phone number, your address, old jobs, and everything else that was never part of the question

@primus_labs is building around that problem.....

you open the real website like normal, with the primus extension in your browser, and zktls turns that logged in session into a proof of one fact

> how it works

- you open the source page yourself
- a primus attestor watches the encrypted session, it never gets your login
- the proof only says one line, like "this credential, true"
- the hiring app gets that proof, not your full file

they never see your private messages, your full history or the rest of the page

the official docs even list this under hiring, proving things like identity, school or skills without handing over the whole file

one limit though.... the proof shows what that website showed at that time, not that the account is uniquely you, and this is a use case for apps to build on, not a primus job board you can apply on today

That's all for now....
