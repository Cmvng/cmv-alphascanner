## Things to know before you post
- **"Sending $eU to a wallet":** I could not find a wallet-to-wallet eU send on the chain yet. The recent eU transfers
  are all between the eU contract and the vault's deposit contract.
  - The send itself is an illustration in the video.
  - The explorer screen is a real eU transfer showing Value 0.
  - If you record your own send (your wallet and the BscTrace page of that transaction), I can put it in that spot.
- **Wrapping shows the amount.** Moving U into eU and back shows the amount, because U is public. Only eU transfers and
  vault positions read 0. Your line "only the amounts are hidden" holds for eU itself.
- **"1:1"** is your wording. Primus's docs describe the one-step conversion, and the wrap and unwrap rows match in
  and out, but I did not find "1:1" written on a Primus page.
- **Kept off screen:** the secondary reports (yield from Unitas, about $1M in the first day, 8-hour batches). I could
  not confirm them on Primus's own pages.
- **BscScan** blocks automated visits, so the screens are BscTrace, NodeReal's BNB Chain explorer, showing the same chain.
- **Music:** "A New Life", your favourite. I can't hear, so the sound was checked by measurement, and every caption was
  read back against the final mix.
- **Reactions:** two stickers, from both sets: Hmm (shirtless) on "I don't think a payment or a savings position…",
  and The Grin (grey) on the sign-off.
