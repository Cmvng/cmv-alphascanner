Real businesses won't bring their assets onchain if they don't know who they are sharing the chain with. MMIX is building a layer 1 where every project gets vetted before it launches. By the way, here is how it works. Companies, asset issuers and validators get reviewed first, then anyone can use the apps built on it, so things like real estate, payments and loyalty can come onchain without the free for all. EVM compatible, so builders use Solidity and the Ethereum tools they already know. Validators are appointed by the MMIX Foundation, 3 planned at launch, with blocks every 2-3 seconds. Compliance built into the chain: an onchain registry for KYC tiers, issuer permissions and transfer rules. Every app runs on MMIX Coin for fees, from a tokenized property, to a neobank account, to an online checkout. So instead of a tokenized property sitting on the same chain as the next rug, every project there has been checked first. There is also a quest hub on hub.mmix.io, where you do tasks, earn points, and work toward the airdrop and NFT eligibility.
