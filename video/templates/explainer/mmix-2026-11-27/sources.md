## Not checked, or worth knowing (for the message, not the screen)
- The name: "MMIX is 2009 in Roman numerals" (faq). No pronunciation was found; Lara says "em-mix".
- The MMIX Foundation is a Panama private-interest foundation, "not licensed or regulated as a financial services
  provider" (disclaimers §12). MMIX Coin carries "no promise… of value", and "We do not sell, offer or exchange MMIX
  Coin" (Terms §1, disclaimers §4).
- No audits yet: "Independent reviews happen before mainnet." There are no public docs, whitepaper or GitHub.
- On 27 Nov copycat "MMIX" tokens appeared on DexScreener (on Robinhood Chain). MMIX does not link to them.
- The MMIX Wallet buttons open "Ducatus Wallet 3.0" (developer Denarius).
- Who can take part: 18+, with sanctions exclusions; no country list.
- The ecosystem names on the cards: Spirit Hills (RWA), X-Gen Node (infrastructure), Denarius and Gateway (financial
  services), Remus Nation (eCommerce), AA+ DEX (DeFi), VIV, Tatooine Travel and SLC Paddle (hospitality).
