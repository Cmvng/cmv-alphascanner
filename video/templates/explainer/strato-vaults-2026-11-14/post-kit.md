- the returns get converted back to ETH, so each carryETH is worth a bit more ETH over time, 1 carryETH is about 1.0046 ETH right now
>>>about $89K in the vault

> wBTC carry vault
- you deposit wBTC and get carryWBTC back
- the vault locks the wBTC as collateral, borrows USDST against it and puts that USDST into yield paying stablecoins like syrupUSDC and sUSDS
- whatever is left after the borrow cost gets converted back to BTC
>>>about $71K in the vault
>>>about 1.39% apy, no reward points on it right now

so the ETH vault is staking yield, while the BTC one is a carry trade, you earn the gap between what the stablecoins pay and what the loan costs

BTW 1 carryWBTC still redeems for exactly 1 wBTC, so it hasn't added any BTC to the shares yet

both keep some idle funds for withdrawals, but a big withdrawal can sit in a queue while the money is deployed

the BTC vault has a loan inside it, so a sharp BTC drop or a stablecoin losing its peg hits it harder
