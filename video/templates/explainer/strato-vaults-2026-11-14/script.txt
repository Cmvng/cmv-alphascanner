If you hold ETH or BTC, Strato has two vaults for them, and they don't earn the same way.

ETH Yield Vault.

You deposit ETH and get carry ETH back.

The vault puts the ETH into strategies like W S T ETH, so it earns ETH staking yield.

The returns get converted back to ETH, so each carry ETH is worth a bit more ETH over time. One carry ETH is about one point zero zero four six ETH right now.

About eighty-nine thousand dollars in the vault. About one point nine percent base A P Y, plus about one percent in reward points.

W BTC Carry Vault.

You deposit W BTC and get carry W BTC back.

The vault locks the W BTC as collateral, borrows U S D S T against it, and puts that U S D S T into yield paying stablecoins, like syrup U S D C and S U S D S.

Whatever is left after the borrow cost gets converted back to BTC.

About seventy-one thousand dollars in the vault. About one point three nine percent A P Y, no reward points on it right now.

So the ETH vault is staking yield, while the BTC one is a carry trade. You earn the gap between what the stablecoins pay, and what the loan costs.

By the way, one carry W BTC still redeems for exactly one W BTC, so it hasn't added any BTC to the shares yet.

Both keep some idle funds for withdrawals, but a big withdrawal can sit in a queue while the money is deployed.

The BTC vault has a loan inside it, so a sharp BTC drop, or a stablecoin losing its peg, hits it harder.

That's all for now.
