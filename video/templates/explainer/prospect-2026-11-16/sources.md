## The owner's claims, checked
1. **"a prediction market built for sports and it is live now": confirmed.**
   - Prospect's release says it is "now live in the United States" and that "Eligible U.S. customers can now trade event contracts at prospectmarkets.com". (Prospect Prediction Markets Inc., Newsfile, 12 Nov 2026 8:00 AM EST: newsfilecorp.com/release/323490, read as served.)
   - Its own post says "PROSPECT MARKETS IS LIVE NOW" (@ProspectMarkets on X, 12 Nov 2026 17:13 UTC, post 2120927919904067886).
   - An invitation-only soft launch ran from Sep 2026.
2. **"every game becomes a market with contracts on the outcome, and the price is set peer to peer by other traders": confirmed in Prospect's own words.**
   - The release says: "Fans trade peer-to-peer on outcomes" and "Prospect turns every sports moment … into a live, tradable market".
   - The order book in Prospect's launch video shows asks and bids with share counts.
   - Crypto.com's help page for CDNA sports contracts says a buy needs "another party selling it to match". Market makers also quote there, and a Prospect affiliate (Prospect Trading USA LLC) is a CDNA member with a market-making business (Covers, 25 Aug 2026). So "peer to peer" is Prospect's description, not "no house".
3. **"50% … reads as the implied probability … the price can move while the game is on": confirmed.**
   - A CDNA sports contract pays US$1 if you are right and nothing if not (Crypto.com Help Center, "Sports Event Trading", read 16 Nov). So a 50¢ price reads as a 50% chance, before fees.
   - The real 50¢ example: Carolina Panthers 50¢ / New Orleans Saints 50¢ (@ProspectMarkets on X, 15 Nov 2026, post 2122016038145695934, "Prices as of 12:12 PM ET").
   - Live prices: the release says "from the opening whistle to the final result, into a live, tradable market", and Prospect's launch video shows the price chart moving in a game.
4. **"the screen also shows real-time information next to the market, play by play and stats": confirmed.**
   - Prospect's post (12 Nov) says: "real-time data, play-by-play and stats in one place".
   - The release adds: "Live data is for informational purposes only. It may be delayed or incorrect."
5. **"listed and cleared on crypto.com's exchange (CDNA), which is CFTC-registered": confirmed.**
   - Exchange: CFTC, Designated Contract Markets 34536, "North American Derivatives Exchange, Inc. d/b/a crypto.com", Status: Designated, 2004-02-18. Shown on screen. (cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/IndustryFilings/TradingOrganizations/34536)
   - Clearinghouse: CFTC, Derivative Clearing Organizations 38, "NADEX d/b/a Crypto.com Derivatives North America", Status: Registered, 2004-02-18. Shown on screen. (cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/IndustryFilings/ClearingOrganizations/38)
   - The release says contracts are "listed and priced on, and cleared by" CDNA, "a CFTC-regulated exchange and clearinghouse".
   - Note: the CFTC calls the exchange "Designated" and the clearinghouse "Registered". The owner's "CFTC-registered" is kept.
6. **"prospect brokerage is the introducing broker that connects traders to it": confirmed by the company.**
   - The release says: "Customers are introduced to Foris DAX FCM, LLC (d/b/a Crypto.com FCM) by Prospect Brokerage USA LLC, an introducing broker registered with the CFTC and a member of the NFA." Shown on screen.
   - The chain on screen: traders, then Prospect Brokerage, then Crypto.com FCM (holds the account), then CDNA.
   - The NFA's own record (ID, status) was not checked: the NFA BASIC search refuses scripted visits. Look up "Prospect Brokerage USA" at nfa.futures.org/BasicNet.
7. **"aimed at US traders and eligibility rules apply": confirmed.**
   - The release says: "Restrictions and eligibility requirements apply. Not available in all states. You must be 18 or older and a U.S. resident."
   - Prospect's own cards add "18+ (or higher where required)".
   - The list of excluded states is not public on a page we could read.
8. **$MKT.V $MKTSF (in the post text, not in the video):** Prospect Prediction Markets Inc. (formerly DeepMarkit Corp.), Vancouver; TSXV: MKT, OTCQB: MKTSF, FSE: DEP (release, 12 Nov).
