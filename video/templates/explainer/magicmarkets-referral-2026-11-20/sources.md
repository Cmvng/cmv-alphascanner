## MagicMarkets
- **The referral, docs (traders):** docs.magicmarkets.com/traders/referral-dividend, last changed 16 Nov 2026.
  - "Invite people who trade, and you earn 10bps of their volume paid in USDT at the end of each month."
  - "You get a referral link from Rewards in your account settings."
  - "What counts: Taker volume from your referred users: the notional value of trades they placed by taking a price
    already on the book."
  - "The dividend follows volume. It does not pay you when your referrals lose money."
  - "The binding version is the Referral Terms … Where the two differ, the terms govern."
- **The binding Referral Terms:** magicmarkets.com/referral-program, "Last Updated: 1st July 2026".
  - §3.1: "Each calendar month, MagicMarkets will establish a dividend pool."
  - §3.2: "The methodology … including the precise formula, rates, and any adjustments — is proprietary to
    MagicMarkets and will not be disclosed. MagicMarkets retains full discretion over the size, composition, and
    distribution of the Dividend Pool."
  - §3.4: each referrer's share is proportional to their referrals' taker volume against all referrers' taker volume.
  - §3.6: credited within ten business days after each calendar month ends.
  - §3.8: "The Dividend Pool may be zero in any given month … There is no guaranteed minimum dividend."
  - §5.2: no representation that the Program will continue for any specified period.
  - §2.4 forbids sharing the link "through paid advertising or any media that implies endorsement by or affiliation
    with MagicMarkets without prior written consent". It also forbids making "any representation about the Program
    or its rewards that MagicMarkets has not expressly authorised".
- **The partner Dividend page:** docs.magicmarkets.com/partners/dividend, last changed 9 Oct 2026. It says:
  "MagicMarkets does not publish a rate, a formula or a percentage for the dividend, and partners do not advertise
  one." This conflicts with the traders' page above.
- **How the exchange works** (docs, last changed 20 Nov): "Orders from other MagicMarkets traders." and "Aggregated
  liquidity from more than 25 sportsbooks, sports exchanges and prediction markets".
- **Fees** (docs): "0% commission on trades. There is no fee to place, match, win or close a trade." The Terms of
  Service (last updated 12 Oct 2026), §11.1, say: "Currently … does not charge any commission … we reserve the right
  to introduce a commission in the future." Hence "for now".
- **Volume:** the DefiLlama API (api.llama.fi/summary/dexs/magicmarkets, read 20 Nov) gives $95.07M over 30 days.
  The figure counts the taker side only and is "Sourced from the team-maintained public Dune dataset". That makes it
  self-reported, so the video says "by its own figures".
- **Operator and licence:** 3-102-947650 SRL, Costa Rica, licence ALSI-202607031-FI1 (docs: company and licence).
  The Anjouan Gaming register (anjouangaming.com/license-register, read 20 Nov) lists it as valid, B2C, issued

- **Not available:** Australia, Austria, China, Comoros, Costa Rica, Curaçao, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Macau,
  Netherlands, Singapore, Spain, the United Kingdom, the United States and its territories, plus sanctioned regions.
  Source: docs, company and licence, and Terms of Service §7.1.3. Age: 18, or the local age of majority if higher.
