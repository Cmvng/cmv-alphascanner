## What is an illustration
- The night scene, the phone and its screens (a tick, three rows, a lock, a speech bubble). They are symbols, not
  Bagel's own screens; the footnote says so. "Your combo won tonight" is an example, with no amounts.
- The Help Center's own example: a win at 10:00 AM UTC+8 is usually claimed on the next day's run. An evening result
  in Lagos falls before the 1:30 AM run; settlement follows Polymarket, so the timing can vary.
