# Matchday Myths: "El Clásico — The Wounded Armada" (story draft, about 90 s)

**Style:** stylised real players: recognisable, in the look of an AAA game cinematic, not photoreal (owner, 23 Oct).

**Narrator:** the Wizard, a recurring original character.

**Facts:** every fact is from `clasico-2026-10-25-sources.md`.

## Cold open: Paris (0:00–0:09)
**Picture:**
- Night rain on a burning Paris bridge.
- Extreme close-up: Lamine Yamal, rain and ash on his face.
- Four fire arrows thud into a mast, and a blue-and-garnet banner drops into a puddle.

**Wizard (low):** "For nine battles, the armada never bled. …Then came Paris."

**Title, on the music's drop:** MATCHDAY MYTHS · EL CLÁSICO: THE WOUNDED ARMADA

## Act 1: The Record (0:09–0:24)
**Picture:**
- Sepia flashback to 1968: white knights in a painted hall, and a golden scroll with nine seals.
- Then the present: the armada sweeps past nine burning watchtowers.
- Raphinha draws a bow, a quiver of arrows on his back.

**Wizard:**
- "Fifty-eight years ago, a white kingdom wrote a record no one could match: nine wars, nine victories."
- "This season, Barcelona matched it. Thirty-nine goals. Twelve from Raphinha's bow alone."

## Act 2: The Wound (0:24–0:38)
**Picture:**
- Out of the fog come navy-and-red ships (no crests); four arrows find the fleet.
- Then a healers' tent, with three knights on cots, captioned Olmo, Eric García, Christensen.

**Wizard:**
- "But on Tuesday, in Paris, four arrows found their mark. Four–two. The first defeat."
- "Three knights still lie in the healers' tent."

## Act 3: The Kingdom (0:38–0:58)
**Picture:**
- On the white citadel's balcony, Mourinho in a long dark coat, a cold half-smile.
- Vinícius behind the bars of a tower cell, watching the army leave.
- Mbappé walks out of the gate alone, a sword on his shoulder and the white army behind him.

**Wizard:**
- "In the white kingdom, an old general is waiting. José Mourinho has broken Barcelona before."
- "His fastest rider, Vinícius, is banished: four battles in chains."
- "So the kingdom sends its champion. Mbappé. Nine goals. Four straight wins. Six points to take back."

## Act 4: The Eve (0:58–1:12)
**Picture:**
- Camp Nou as a fortress at night. Flick in a war tent, leaning over a map.
- Intercut extreme close-ups of Yamal's eyes and Mbappé's eyes, then the two armies across the field.

**Wizard:**
- "'You don't win titles in October,' says the armada's general."
- "But one more win… and a record that has stood since 1968 belongs to Barcelona alone."

## The Prophecy (1:12–1:30)
**Picture:**
- The Wizard's tower in a storm. His staff ignites, and the smoke forms Camp Nou.
- Card: THE PROPHECY: BARCELONA DON'T LOSE · Sunday 9 p.m. Lagos

**Wizard:**
- "The wizard has seen how it ends."
- (whisper) "The armada… does not fall."
- "Follow. And see if the prophecy holds."

## Production plan
**About 22 shots:**
- **8 hero shots:** the Paris ambush, Yamal's close-up, the Mourinho balcony, Vinícius in the cell, Mbappé leaving the gate, the eyes intercut, the Wizard's staff, the stand-off. Each on Kling 3.0, two takes, keeping the best.
- **The other 14:** Veo 3.1 Lite, or stills with camera moves.

**Estimate:** about 75,000–80,000 credits.

**First:** a look test of 6 stylised character stills, about 1,650 credits.
