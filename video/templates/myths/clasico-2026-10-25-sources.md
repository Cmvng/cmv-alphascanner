## The verdict
**"The armada does not fall" means Barcelona don't lose.** The reasons:
- They are at home.
- They have the league's best attack: 39 goals, and Raphinha has 12.
- They won the last two Clásicos.
- Real Madrid are without Vinícius and Rodrygo.

The risk is Barcelona's defence: they are missing Eric García and Christensen, Koundé is doubtful, and PSG scored four against them on Tuesday.
