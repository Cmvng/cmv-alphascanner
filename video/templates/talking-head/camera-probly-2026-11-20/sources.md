## Probly
- **Site:** probly.com. Its own line: "PROBLY is a prediction market platform where you can trade on the outcomes of
  real-world events across politics, sports, crypto, finance and more." Tagline: "Before It Happens".
- **The categories** (front page, captured): Crypto, Sports, Esports, Elections, Politics, Finance, and more; a
  Pop-Culture market ("Next CEO of NVIDIA?") at the top.
- **The promo, "Safety Net"** (probly.com/en/safety-net, captured), verbatim:
  - "Safety Net. Your losses on us. Trade $50+ across 3 days. End the campaign with a net loss and we return up to $25
    of it as a trading bonus. Nov 12, 2026 - Nov 24, 2026 (12:00 UTC)"
  - The steps:
    1. "Join the campaign. Click "Join Now". Only trades you make after joining count."
    2. "Trade $50+ on 3 different days. Trade at least once on any 3 days and reach $50 in total trading volume
       during the campaign period… Only taker trades count".
    3. "Get up to $25 back on losses. End the campaign with a realised net loss and we return up to $25 of it as a
       trading bonus. Distributed to your Reward Center within 3 days after the campaign ends."
  - The rules (same page):
    - "The Bonus is non-withdrawable, non-transferable, carries no cash value, and is valid for seven (7) days".
    - It must be used on a single market order, priced between 15¢ and 85¢.
    - The maximum payout is 200% of the bonus.
    - One bonus per person. The pool is $2,000 in total, "first-come first-served".
    - Only trades opened and closed within the campaign count as realised losses.
- **Deposits** (docs.probly.com/get-started/deposits-and-withdrawals, captured):
  - Deposits are in USDC through Fun.xyz.
  - "A) Connected Wallet – Transfer available funds directly from your connected wallet. Any required conversion is
    handled automatically. B) Transfer Crypto – Copy your deposit address and send any of the supported crypto from
    another wallet."
  - The networks the page names (withdrawal fees): Arbitrum, Base, Polygon PoS, Ethereum, Solana.
  - The deposit page that lists the accepted networks needs a login.
- **Who runs it:** WisdomBayes Inc. (Terms of Use, last updated April 2026), under Panama law. It runs on TxFlow L1.
  No licence, team or audit is named.
- **Restricted** (Terms): the US, the UK, France, Canada, Singapore, Poland, the Netherlands, Thailand, China and Hong
  Kong, Hungary, Portugal, Brazil, Argentina, sanctioned regions, and others "not limited to". Nigeria is not named.
  Users must be 18 or older.

## The owner's words against the pages (said in the message; their words are kept on screen)
- "earn a trading bonus up to $25", "trade $50", "in any 3 days", "ends on the 24th of November": all match the page.
- **These do not match the page:**
  - "Put $50 on Bayern... they win, I get $25 in bonus." The $25 comes back only on a net LOSS at the end of the
    campaign.
  - "I use that $25 to put it on Arsenal... and I withdraw." The bonus is paid within 3 days AFTER 24 Nov, so it is
    too late for Saturday. It cannot be withdrawn, only traded, and only its winnings can be paid out.
  - "from any chain": deposits are USDC. The connected-wallet route converts other funds, but the accepted networks
    sit behind a login.
- Not said, and worth knowing:
  - Click "Join Now" first, or trades don't count.
  - The pool is $2,000, first come, first served.
- Probly's Bayern market carries the same ID and price as Polymarket's public market (6771192). Probly does not say
  that it mirrors Polymarket, so nothing on screen says so.
