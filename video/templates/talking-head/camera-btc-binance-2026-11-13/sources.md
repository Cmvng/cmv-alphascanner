## The hacks
- Bybit, 21 Feb 2025, about $1.5B (≈401,000 ETH; Arkham: $1.46B). CNBC, 21 Feb 2025: "Hackers steal $1.5 billion from
  exchange Bybit in biggest-ever crypto heist". FBI: North Korea (Lazarus / TraderTraitor).
- How Bybit filled it: Bitget lent 40,000 ETH, no interest, repaid by 25 Feb (Cointelegraph, 25 Feb 2025); bridge
  loans from partners incl. Galaxy Digital, FalconX, Wintermute; gap closed, CEO Ben Zhou, 24 Feb 2025 (CNBC; The Block).
- Binance, 29 Oct 2026, about $6.3B (Binance: $6.34B); the largest crypto hack on record (Bloomberg, 3 Nov 2026:
  "record heist"; CNBC 30 Oct and 3 Nov 2026). FBI: North Korea's TraderTraitor; North Korea denies it.
- How Binance covered it: SAFU emergency fund, the Futures insurance fund above its required level, the corporate
  treasury; SAFU to be refilled to about $1B (Korea Herald, 13 Nov 2026; Bloomberg via Quartz, 12 Nov 2026).
  "Binance did not borrow money": CZ on X, 2 Nov 2026 (one source, his own claim).
- Frozen by partners (The Block, 30 Oct 2026): Tether over $1.57B in USDT; Circle over $800M in USDC ($823M per Crypto
  Briefing, 29 Oct); Ripple about $200M; World Liberty Financial about $29M; about $3B in all. US authorities and
  companies together: more than $2.7B frozen; DOJ cleared to seize more than $1.2B of it (The Block; CoinDesk, 12 Nov 2026).
