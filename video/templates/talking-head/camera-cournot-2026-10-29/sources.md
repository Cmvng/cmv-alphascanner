## The owner's post, checked

- **"Verification and privacy layer":** the 29 Oct post's "About Primus Labs" says "a verification and privacy layer built
  with zkTLS and FHE". Primus's docs say "a trust, verification, and privacy layer".
- **"Without showing your login or the rest of the page"** is the owner's plain-words summary.
  - It is not a Primus quote.
  - Primus says "verify data authenticity without revealing the data itself".
  - Cournot says "Evidence authenticity, without the contents".
- **How Cournot settles:** reads the market's own rules, gathers evidence and returns a verdict with its reasoning trace
  (cournot.ai). "Anyone can bond and re-run a claim": a challenger has to post a bond.
- **The strict part:** both rules are confirmed in the docs (quoted above). They are Cournot's stated design. The 29 Oct
  post says Cournot "can use" Primus for some markets.
- **42:** 42.space, "an asset issuance protocol for future events", on BNB Chain. Cournot: "Official resolution layer for
  42". Chainwire press release, 15 Jun 2026.
- **The figures** are Cournot's own, with no independent check. 99.71% + 0.30% = 100.01% (rounding).
- **The caveat** ("the proof shows the data came from the source, not that the source was right") is the owner's. It is
  supported by Cournot's docs: outputs "are not represented as proving that a natural-language judgment is semantically
  true". Also by Oasis, 17 Mar 2025: zkTLS "makes no claims about truth".
