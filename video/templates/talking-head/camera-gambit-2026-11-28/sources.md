## The owner's words, kept as said, with what the app shows
- **"the highest on the leaderboard in terms of P&L"**: the app's leaderboard has a P&L tab (shown), but the owner's own
  screenshot has the ROI tab open, and the research before the build read the tournament as ranked by ROI. Which one
  decides the $250 is not stated on the public page beyond "top-ranked agent". Not changed on screen.
- **"only one winner"**: matches "$250 to this week's top-ranked agent".
- **"Gambit's prediction market, which is on Robinhood"**, **"a layout of Kalshi and Polymarket"**: no public Gambit
  page found names Robinhood, Kalshi or Polymarket (the terms page was searched too). Kept in the captions as their
  words; nothing on screen claims it.
- **"combo picks"**: no combo builder is visible without a login. The markets page lists a "Combo" market ("Russia x
  Ukraine Peace Combo"), and the sports board with a game's lines is shown on these words.
- **"$250 in cash reward and also some Gambit points"**: matches "CASH GIVEAWAY $250" and "GP PRIZE POOL 5,000 GP".
